# Field barrier audit protocol (draft)

Draft written 2026-11-17. **No audit has been carried out.** This is what a team audit of the pilot area (`PILOT_AREA.md`) would follow. Revise it after the first session and after talking to local disability organizations.

## Before going out

- Print or load the list of barriers to check: the 20 OSM `kerb=raised` barriers, the crossing ends with no kerb tag, and any pending community reports. Every map has a list equivalent.
- Work in pairs, in daylight, with a phone camera. Do not enter private property or stand in the roadway to take a photo.

## At each location

1. Confirm you are at the right point (coordinates within about 10 m).
2. Record the barrier type with the app's types: `missing_curb_ramp`, `surface_problem`, `obstruction`, `no_sidewalk`, `steep_slope`. Or record "no barrier found".
3. Severity 1–5, using these anchors: 5 = impassable for a wheelchair user; 3 = passable with difficulty or a detour of less than one block; 1 = minor.
4. Take a photo that shows the barrier and does not show faces or licence plates. Note the time.
5. For a raised kerb: is there a ramp anywhere on that corner? Is it within the crosswalk?

## Recording

- Enter findings as field verification evidence: the reviewer's reason names the date, the auditor pair and what was seen.
- Until photo upload with location-metadata removal is built (see `METHODOLOGY_AND_PRIVACY.md` §4), keep photos in the team's private storage. Record only a reference to each photo.
- Report every checked location, including "no barrier found". Silence must never be read as "accessible".
